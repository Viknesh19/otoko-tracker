# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Otoko Tracker is for anime, movie, manga, and manhwa viewers who want a fast local-first way to search AniList, save titles, and update progress without creating a hosted account.

## Product Purpose

The product helps users maintain a personal viewing and reading library on one device. Success means users can quickly discover a title, understand its release/progress context, add it to a collection, update progress, and trust that the data stays private in the browser.

## Brand Personality

Focused, cinematic, precise. The interface should feel like a premium media command surface: atmospheric enough for entertainment, but still practical for repeated tracking.

## Anti-references

Avoid gaming-dashboard clutter, childish neon, crypto-style glow, generic glass cards everywhere, emoji-driven controls, and visual effects that slow down core tracking tasks.

## Design Principles

- Put collection status and next action first.
- Let cover art provide the media texture while the UI stays calm and legible.
- Use motion to clarify state changes, not to decorate every surface.
- Preserve local-first trust through clear privacy and data controls.
- Keep discovery, details, and progress editing reachable on small screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus states, semantic form labels, readable mobile text, touch targets of at least 44px, and reduced-motion fallbacks for all decorative movement.
